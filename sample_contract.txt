EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2024, by and between TechCorp Inc. ("Company") and John Doe ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee shall serve as Software Developer. Employee agrees to devote full-time efforts to the Company and shall not engage in any other employment without prior written consent.

2. COMPENSATION
Employee shall receive a base salary of $85,000 per year, payable bi-weekly. The Company reserves the right to modify compensation at its sole discretion at any time without prior notice.

3. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
Upon termination, Employee shall not work for any competitor or start a competing business within a 200-mile radius for a period of 3 years. Violation of this clause will result in liquidated damages of $500,000.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work, ideas, inventions, and creations developed by Employee during the term of employment, whether during working hours or personal time, shall be the exclusive property of the Company.

5. TERMINATION
The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, with or without cause, and without prior notice. Employee must provide 90 days written notice before resignation.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of all company information for a period of 10 years following termination.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in a location chosen by the Company. Employee waives all rights to jury trial.

8. BENEFITS
Employee shall be eligible for standard company benefits including health insurance, 401(k) matching, and 15 days paid time off per year.

9. WORK HOURS
Standard work hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Reasonable overtime may be required.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
